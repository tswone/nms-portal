# Project brief (summary)

A paraphrased summary of the work sample brief. The original brief is not published here.

## Task

Build a prototype **parent portal** for National Math Stars in about two hours, using dummy data. In production, family and program data live in Zoho (CRM) and financial data in Ramp and QuickBooks.

The brief does not expect a complete product. The goal is to show what can be built in the time.

## The portal should

- Let parents see and update basic information (school, address, contact info)
- Give them an interactive checklist of to-dos (e.g. forms to fill out)
- Show their Star's program progress and history (courses, competitions, summer camps)
- Show what the family budget has been spent on, and how much remains
- Let them browse upcoming virtual and in-person events and RSVP, and remind them of events they've already RSVP'd to
- Nudge parents to complete required actions they're behind on

## It could also

- Offer advice on suitable opportunities for a Star
- Answer common parent questions
- Host self-service resources (e.g. guides to grade acceleration)
- Serve as an interactive badgebook / pinbook for the merit badges program
- Host discussion forums

## Deliverables

- A live link to the product and the codebase
- An unedited screen recording of the build
- A one-page write-up: (A) the most important questions and how to answer them, (B) the biggest architecture or design choice and why, (C) what to do next
